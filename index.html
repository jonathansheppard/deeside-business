Good news from Deeside's businesses

Local and regional papers used to run strong business pages. As newsrooms have shrunk and the chase for clicks has taken over, business stories have become mostly about failure: closures, job losses, firms going under.

Deeside Business is about the other side. New jobs, contract wins, openings, investment, apprentices and awards from firms across Deeside and Flintshire, home to the biggest concentration of industry in North Wales. It's free and open to every business, large or small.

How it works

A Deeside.com journalist reads every submission, checks the facts and writes or edits the story in our style. Good news goes on Deeside.com as well as here, so it reaches our full readership.

Sending a story doesn't guarantee we'll publish it, and nothing here is paid for. Advertising and sponsored content on Deeside.com are always labelled.

About Deeside.com

Deeside.com is independent local news for Deeside and Flintshire. Alongside the news we run live tools for the area, from fuel prices and planning applications to food hygiene ratings and traffic.
